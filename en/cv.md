Bachelor of Law and Social Sciences (LL.B.), Faculty of Law, Universidad de Chile (1990). Lawyer.

## Teaching and professional experience

- Professor of Law, Universidad Adolfo Ibáñez, from 2010 to present.
- Associate Professor of Law, 2005-2009.
- Assistant Professor of Law, 1997-2005.
- Academic Director of the Master's Degree in Labor Law and Social Security (MDLS-UAI).
- Professor since 1997 in several postgraduate and master's in law courses. Professor at the Judicial Studies Institute and the Judicial Academy.
- Legal Advisor of the Chilean Senate (1991-2005).

## Publications

- 18 labor law books published in Chile, Uruguay, Brazil and the USA, among others:
    - *Introducción al Derecho del Trabajo* (ConoSur, 1998)
    - *Cidadania na Empresa e Eficácia Diagonal dos Direitos Fundamentais* (Editora LTR, 2011)
    - *Principled Labor Law. U.S. Labor Law through a Latin American Method*, co-authored with César F. Rosado Marzán (Oxford University Press, 2019)
    - *Derecho Colectivo del Trabajo* (DER, 3rd ed., 2020)
    - *Derecho Individual del Trabajo. Doctrina, materiales y casos* (DER, 2021)
- One hundred chapters and papers in law journals, among others: *Economic and Industrial Democracy*, *Comparative Labor Law & Policy Journal*, *Employee Rights and Employment Policy Journal*, *Hofstra Labor & Employment Law Journal*, *International Journal of Comparative Labour Law and Industrial Relations*, *Washington University Global Studies Law Review*, *Latin American Legal Studies*, *Boletín Mexicano de Derecho Comparado*, *Revista de la Facultad de Derecho de México*, *Diritto delle Relazioni Industriali*, *Revue de Droit Comparé du Travail et de la Sécurité Sociale* and *Revue de Droit du Travail*.

See the [full bibliography]({{ '/en/publications/' | relative_url }}).
